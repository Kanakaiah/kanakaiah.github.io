# Deuteronomy chapter image prompts — revised for recognition

Revised November 19, 2026 following the user’s request. All 34 chapters use concrete visual anchors, checked against the relevant passages in the bundled BSB text. Ezekiel’s object/action anchors (wheel, scroll, watchman, divided hair) are the design reference. Chapter numbers and keywords remain in the existing app UI; the user subsequently requested inscriptions on the tablets in chapters 5, 9, and 10. Ezekiel itself and app memory anchors are unchanged.

Each picture is a representative chapter cue, not a claim to show every verse. Composite and metaphorical scenes are explicitly identified. Recognition must come from the scene itself; the existing app supplies chapter labels and memory keywords.

The approved set is at `public/chapters/deuteronomy/chN.png`; matching JPEGs serve the Android reader. Review drafts are in `artwork-review/deuteronomy-generated-v3/`. Structured scene notes: `prompts/deuteronomy-scenes.json`.

Review every image for: required objects/actions; distinction from adjacent chapters; no chapter labels, numbers, or invented readable scripture; engraved artistic marks on the tablets in chapters 5, 9 and 10; coherent anatomy; important subjects visible in full-bleed landscape composition. Regenerate failures before recommending the set for use.

## Important corrections

- 2: peaceful passage and Sihon’s defeat are distinct stages.
- 6: household teaching and doorpost reminders make love/obedience concrete.
- 5/9/10: tablets presented, tablets broken, new tablets stored are different images.
- 13/18: rejecting an idol-leading false prophet versus welcoming the promised prophet.
- 14/26: a tenth share versus the firstfruits basket.
- 24: include protection of workers and gleaners, beyond divorce alone.
- 27: the law is written on plastered stones; curses are proclaimed.
- 29: communal covenant renewal is primary, not a mysterious secret.
- 31/32: handover and written witness versus the song’s rock/eagle imagery.
- 34: the view before death; no invented known burial monument.

Source: the project’s `android/app/src/main/assets/bible.db` (book 5), relevant chapter passages cited below; [BSB Deuteronomy](https://biblehub.com/bsb/deuteronomy/1.htm), including [6](https://biblehub.com/bsb/deuteronomy/6.htm), [10](https://biblehub.com/bsb/deuteronomy/10.htm), [29](https://biblehub.com/bsb/deuteronomy/29.htm), and [31](https://biblehub.com/bsb/deuteronomy/31.htm).
