# Genesis — validated chapter-image prompts

Adapted from the supplied Downloads file against the Genesis text and the app’s existing chapter keywords. Scenes are mnemonic illustrations, not photographs or claims to show every event in a chapter. Separate moments are labeled as vignettes or symbolic compositions. Chapter number and keyword belong in the app UI, not on artwork.

Corrections include Abraham’s laughter in chapter 17 versus Sarah’s in chapter 18; removing a claimed direction of smoke and weapon from chapter 4; the well in chapter 21; distinct times and locations for chapters 12, 24 and 31; and Joseph’s coat removed before the pit in chapter 37. The chapter 50 funeral and Joseph’s coffin are separate events.

Approved images are in `public/chapters/genesis/chN.png`; JPEG copies serve the Android reader. Review images are retained in `artwork-review/genesis-generated/`. Source: supplied prompt file; the project’s `android/app/src/main/assets/bible.db` (Genesis, book 1); `src/data/otGuides/genesis.ts`.
