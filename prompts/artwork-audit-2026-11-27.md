# Existing chapter artwork review — 2026-11-27

Reviewed 486 existing chapter illustrations across 33 books: Genesis, Exodus, Deuteronomy, Job, Ezekiel, Daniel, and all 27 New Testament books. The other 703 Old Testament chapter illustrations were completed in the preceding task and are outside this review.

## Result

- Retained 460 illustrations; corrected 26: Job 5, Ezekiel 15, Romans 6.
- Revised all 106 chapter prompts for Job, Ezekiel and Romans, adding passage references and clarifying metaphors, visions and narrated events.
- Retained all 22 Revelation illustrations. Full-size checks of chapters 5, 12 and 21 did not identify a count correction.
- Kept chapter numbers and keywords in the app interface, outside the artwork.
- Used built-in image generation for replacements and targeted edits; all 26 outputs were visually reviewed after generation.

## Method

Contact-sheet visual inspection of each chapter, full-size inspection for ambiguous details and edit targets, comparison with chapter-specific prompts and bundled Bible passages. This is an editorial visual review, not a theological or archaeological certification.

Compared the existing illustrations with their chapter prompts, the app’s chapter cues and selected passages in the bundled English Bible. The artwork is a memory aid: a single scene need not include every event in a chapter. Deliberate metaphor and visionary imagery were retained when they communicate the chapter, rather than treated as ordinary historical events.

Notable prompt-only corrections: Job 5 preserves the negation that trouble does not spring from the ground; Romans 15 uses its travel and collection plans rather than the dividing-wall language from Ephesians 2. Ezekiel 37 explicitly identifies its combination of the bones and two sticks as a poetic composition.
