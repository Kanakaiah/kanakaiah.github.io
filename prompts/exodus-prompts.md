# Exodus — validated chapter-image prompts

Adapted from the supplied Downloads file against the bundled BSB text and current app keywords. Each scene is a chapter memory cue, not a claim to illustrate every verse. Each image focuses on one clear scene. Chapter numbers and keywords belong in the app UI, not on the images; chapters 32 and 34 have artistic tablet inscriptions at the user’s prior request.

Key corrections: chapter 1 depicts forced brick labor; bricks without supplied straw belong to chapter 5. Chapter 8’s explicit Goshen distinction concerns flies. Chapter 17 prioritizes water from the rock, the existing app keyword. Chapter 20 depicts the spoken commandments without the later tablets. Chapter 23 emphasizes Sabbath rest; chapter 24 emphasizes covenant blood; chapter 36 emphasizes making curtains. Chapter 38 references bronze mirrors without inventing a specific melting process.

App-data discrepancy for later review: the Exodus 1 keyword BRICKS is appropriate, but its current subtitle says “No straw for bricks,” which belongs to chapter 5. This draft does not silently change the app data.
