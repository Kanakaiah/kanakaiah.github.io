compiled and indexed following https://github.com/trekhleb/bible-cross-references (MIT License).

   OpenBible.info Cross References (https://www.openbible.info/labs/cross-references/),

   (https://github.com/ scrollmapper / crossreferences).

2. Original Hebrew & Greek Words (stepbible.json)
   STEP Bible — TAHOT (Translators Amalgamated Hebrew OT) and TAGNT (Translators
   Amalgamated Greek NT), based on work at Tyndale House, Cambridge
   (https://www.stepbible.org, https://github.com/STEPBible/STEPBible-Data).
   Reduced to dictionary forms; words used in more than 100 verses are omitted.
